# Paradise Hearts domain

Paradise Hearts is a seasonal reality-dating game. This glossary defines domain terms whose meaning affects game state or player presentation.

## Daily storytelling

**Daily Recap**:
The end-of-day account of the player's story and the off-screen resort storylines that reached them.

**Your day**:
The Daily Recap section for the player's own notable choices, conversations, and consequences.
_Avoid_: While you were busy

**While you were busy**:
The Daily Recap section for off-screen Heartbreaker storylines that the player did not directly experience.
_Avoid_: Your day, daily summary

**Whisper**:
A player-legible account of an off-screen Heartbreaker storyline that reaches the player through the resort's social network.
_Avoid_: System memory, procedural announcement

## Structured play

**Challenge**:
A player-facing competition in the Paradise Hearts season.

**Minigame**:
The deterministic game format that resolves a Challenge through rounds, legal actions, scoring, reveals, and a result.
_Avoid_: Challenge when naming the implementation format
