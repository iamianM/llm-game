## Love Island Term Mapping

### 1. Islanders (Contestants)

**Original:** Islanders
**Problem:** Too directly tied to Love Island brand

**Options:**

| Alternative | Vibe | Notes |
|-------------|------|-------|
| **Guests** | Resort/hospitality | Implies temporary stay, fits villa setting |
| **Residents** | Living community | More permanent, suggests investment |
| **Paramours** | Romantic, literary | Elevated, suggests romance-focused |
| **Singles** | Direct, dating app | Clear purpose, familiar |
| **Competitors** | Game-focused | Emphasizes strategic elements |
| **Romantics** | Emotional | Suggests genuine connection seekers |
| **Seekers** | Quest-like | Implies active search for love |
| **Players** | Strategic | Double meaning (game + dating) |
| **Heartbreakers** | Drama-focused | Fun, cheeky |
| **Contenders** | Competitive | Shows stakes |
| **Suitors** | Classical romance | Timeless feel |
| **Stars** | Celebrity angle | Reality TV vibe |
| **Cast Members** | Reality TV | Acknowledges genre |
| **Hopefuls** | Vulnerable | Suggests genuine desire |
| **Daters** | Simple, clear | No pretense |

**Recommended:** **Guests** (if resort theme) or **Romantics** (if emotional theme)

## Brand Strategy Recommendations

### Understanding the Market Position

### The Four Pillars of Good Game Branding

**1. Clarity (What is it?)**
- Name should communicate genre within 3 seconds
- Player should know: dating sim, social gameplay, romance focus
- Avoid obscure references

**2. Differentiation (What makes it unique?)**
- Must NOT sound like Love Island clone
- Should hint at unique selling point (AI, procedural, replayability)
- Memorable and distinct

**3. Aspirational (Why should I want it?)**
- Name evokes positive emotions
- Suggests escape, fun, romance, drama
- Makes player want to enter that world

**4. Scalable (Can it grow?)**
- Works across platforms (mobile, PC, console)
- Allows for sequels/expansions
- Merchandisable
- Streamable/shareable
